How it fits in the app

  • When: once, on the first launch, right after name and grade.
  • Three starters, one from each part of the islands: Tarsi from the forest, Pawi from the sea, and Haribon from the sky. Each one says a real fact about itself.
  • Everywhere Tarsi appears now, the chosen partner appears instead: home, the startup loading screen, the stage loader, the peekaboo exit and stage-leave cards, the goodbye screen, and the Shop dressing room.
  • Outfits fit all three. The salakot sits on Haribon’s crest, glasses sit on Pawi’s face, and so on.
  • Growing up works the same: Baby → Young → Grown → Elder as the kid answers questions.
  • The partner can still be found in the wild. A kid with Pawi still catches a sea turtle in the Ocean stages for the Dex.
One thing to decide

Students already playing have Tarsi. They could keep Tarsi, or see this picker once the next time they open the app. Their progress stays either way.
