Partner tap moves

Right now, tapping a partner makes it hop. These give Pawi a swim and Haribon a flight instead. Tap any scene (or its Play button) to see it. Each one ends back in its spot, so the home screen layout stays the same.

Pawi swims

Option ARecommended

Swim lap

Paddles up in a big loop and glides back down, leaving a trail of bubbles. About 2 seconds.

Option B

Bubble float

Paddles in place, floats up, bobs, and sinks back down. Quick and gentle.

Option C

Barrel roll

Swims up, does a full spin, and lands with a splash of bubbles. The silliest one.

Haribon flies

Option ARecommended

Flap and hover

Flaps its big wings, lifts off the branch, hovers, and lands. A feather drifts down.

Option B

Big swoop

Takes off, flies out one side, and comes back around from the other to land. The most “flying,” but the longest.

Option C

Mane show

Puffs up its crest, spreads its wings wide, and calls “Kree!” like a real Philippine eagle.

Why A for both: they stay inside the scene, last under 2 seconds, and still look like real swimming and flying. Tarsi keeps its hop, or could get a big tree leap to match.

Tapping again while a move is playing restarts it, so kids who tap fast still see something happen.
