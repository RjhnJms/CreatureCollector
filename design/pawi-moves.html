New moves for Pawi

Three new tap moves for Pawi. Two of them also show something real about sea turtles. Tap a scene, or its Play button, to see it.

Picked Haribon: Flap and hover (Option A)
Option DRecommended

Up for air

Swims up to the surface, pokes its head out, takes a big breath with a “puff!”, and glides back down.

Teaches: sea turtles breathe air, so they swim up to the surface.

Option E

Seagrass snack

Swims over to the seagrass, takes a few bites (“nom nom!”), and swims back to its spot.

Teaches: green sea turtles eat seagrass.

Option F

Zoom dash

Paddles super fast, zooms off one side, and comes zipping back in from the other.

Just for fun.

Why D: it’s clearly swimming, it stays in the scene, and it shows a real fact without any words to read.

Why there’s no “hide in its shell” move: sea turtles can’t pull their heads into their shells, so that move would teach kids something wrong.
