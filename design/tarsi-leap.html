Tarsi’s tree leap

Right now, tapping Tarsi makes it hop. These give it a real tarsier leap, to match Pawi’s swim and Haribon’s flight. Tap a scene, or its Play button, to see it.

Option ARecommended

Trunk cling

Leaps across to a tree trunk, clings on, looks left and right with its big eyes, then leaps back to the branch.

Teaches: tarsiers cling to upright tree trunks and leap from tree to tree.

Option B

Backflip

Springs straight up, does a full backflip, and sticks the landing. Short, and needs no extra trees.

Just for fun.

Option C

Branch to branch

Leaps up to a higher branch, lands, looks around, and leaps back down.

Teaches: tarsiers can leap many times their own body length.

What changes on the home screen: for A, a tree trunk is added at the side of Tarsi’s scene (only when Tarsi is the partner). C would add a small branch instead. B needs nothing new.

Why A: it’s what real tarsiers do, and it gives Tarsi a move as special as Pawi’s and Haribon’s.
