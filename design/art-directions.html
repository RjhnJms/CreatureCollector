Option 4

Glow forest

A magical forest where plants and mushrooms glow in the dark. Creatures have a bright rim light, like in a fantasy adventure game. The most "video game" of the four.

  • Creatures: shaded with glowing outlines and bright eyes
  • Scenes: glowing mushrooms, vines, and floating sparks
  • Buttons: dark tiles that glow when correct
Cute: MediumReadable: MediumEffort: Medium

Note: real tarsiers don't glow. Fireflies and some mushrooms do, so we'd keep the glow on plants and lights and away from the animals' bodies, so kids aren't taught wrong facts.

Option 5

Low-poly twilight

Everything is built from flat-colored facets, like a gem or folded paper, with a warm twilight sky. It feels modern and is very cheap to draw: no textures, only shapes.

  • Creatures: faceted shapes in 2–3 tones each
  • Scenes: triangle hills and trees, sunset-to-night gradient
  • Buttons: slanted flat tiles

Inspired by Alba: A Wildlife Adventure, a wildlife game praised for simple flat-colored animals that are still easy to tell apart (review).
