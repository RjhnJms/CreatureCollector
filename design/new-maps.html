New maps plan

Three new maps join the Rainforest and Ocean: the two "coming soon" ones (Mangrove and Cave) and a new daytime map, the Rice Terraces. Each has its own paper scene, 6 creatures with real day/night habits, 3 stages plus a review, and 10 questions per creature, like the maps you have now. All animals are found in the Philippines.

Rice Terraces

☀️ Daytime map

Mountain steps of green rice, mossy forest edges, and bright birds and butterflies. Mostly day animals, so most searches use the magnifying glass.

What building all three involves
  • 3 new paper scenes, each with day and night versions (Cave is dark inside either way)
  • 18 new creatures drawn in the cute paper-craft style
  • 12 new stages (3 + a review per map), with map cards and trails
  • 180 new questions (10 per creature)
  • Map order: Rainforest → Ocean → Rice Terraces → Mangrove → Cave, each unlocked by finishing the one before

That's about as much as the whole current game, so I'd build one map at a time and show you each before moving on. The facts come from general knowledge, so it's worth a quick check with your teacher. Where I wasn't sure of a Filipino name I put "—" instead of guessing; you or your teacher can fill those in.
