Daytime home, with banderitas

From 6 am to 6 pm the home screen switches to a sunny scene, and the parols become banderitas, the triangle fiesta flags. After 6 pm it goes back to the night scene with parols. Everything else (cards, buttons, tab bar) stays the same.

How it would work

Follows the clock

Day look from 6 am to 6 pm, night look the rest of the time. The greeting already switches between umaga, hapon and gabi.

Your parol colors carry over

The banderitas use the colors of the parol style picked in the Shop, so those purchases still show by day.

Night partners nap by day

Tarsi and other night animals doze on their branch in daylight. Tap to wake them, and they share why they sleep by day. Day animals like Haribon are wide awake.

Same game underneath

Only the scene behind the cards changes. Text, buttons and the tab bar stay exactly where they are.
