Background music options

Six calm tracks from OpenGameArt, all released as CC0: free to use with no credit needed. Press play to listen. They stream from OpenGameArt, so you need internet.

The descriptions come from each track's tags and its author's notes; I can't listen to audio myself, so trust your ears over my blurbs. In the app the music would play quietly under everything, get quieter during questions, and have a mute button.
