Day animals, mockup

Adding Day and Day & night as active times, so creatures that aren't really dawn or dusk animals get honest labels. The game keeps its night-ranger feel: night animals still use the flashlight.

3. New active times

Based on general knowledge, so it's worth double-checking with your teacher or a reference book.

Result: 5 night, 3 day, 4 day & night. "Dawn" and "Dusk" stay in the code for future animals, but no current creature uses them. The partners Haribon and Pawi keep their own home scenes, unchanged.

4. Badges

Night owlCatch 3 creatures that come out at night.
Unchanged. Day & night creatures count too.
Early bird → Sun seekerCatch 3 creatures that come out by day.
"Early bird" needs dawn creatures, and none would be left. Day & night creatures count too. Anyone who already earned Early bird keeps it, renamed.
