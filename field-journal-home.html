Daily home page

  1. Journal cover info: the kid's name, grade, and ranger rank as a stamp.
  2. Streak: days of the week filled in like a hand-drawn calendar.
  3. Partner creature: a taped photo and how close it is to evolving.
  4. Sticky note: 3 daily tasks for a gem reward.
  5. Continue: jumps straight to the next stage.
  6. Night card: appears only after a habitat is finished.
  7. Notebook tabs: Home, Map, Dex, and Badges.

Night expedition unlocks

  1. Sunset animation: the sun sinks and the moon rises.
  2. The reason: tells kids why it unlocked (they finished the habitat).
  3. Mystery clues: hints like "big eyes" for the tarsier, "wings" for the bat, and "glows" for fireflies.
  4. Start: opens the dark flashlight stage.
